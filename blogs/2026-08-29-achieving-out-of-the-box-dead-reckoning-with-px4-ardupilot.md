A practical path to GNSS-resilient navigation using Pixhawk-class flight controllers. By Dr. Walter Stockwell, VP Aerial Solutions, ANELLO Photonics The integration idea is simple: add the ANELLO X3 IMU to a Pixhawk-class autopilot, merge the ANELLO driver, connect over UART, and let the autopilot EKF use the X3 as its primary IMU for GNSS-denied inertial holdover.
