What is a six axis IMU? A six-axis IMU, or six-axis Inertial Measurement Unit, is a motion sensing device that measures linear acceleration and angular velocity along three orthogonal axes. It combines a three-axis accelerometer with a three-axis gyroscope, giving systems the ability to detect linear acceleration and angular velocity (rotation rate) in real time.
